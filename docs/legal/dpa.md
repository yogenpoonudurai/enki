# Data processing addendum (template)

> **Draft — not legal advice.** This template is for Enterprise customers.
> Have counsel review and complete it before signing.

This Data Processing Addendum (“DPA”) forms part of the agreement between
[LEGAL ENTITY] (“Processor”) and [CUSTOMER LEGAL NAME] (“Controller”) for the
hosted enki service.

## 1. Scope and roles

Controller determines the purposes of processing; Processor processes personal
data on Controller's documented instructions to provide the Service. The
subject matter is hosted triage and account management.

## 2. Processing details

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Duration | Term of the agreement plus deletion period |
| Nature and purpose | Hosted triage, allowance management, billing, support |
| Data categories | Account/token metadata, audit metadata, redacted triage payloads, support messages |
| Data subjects | Controller's personnel and users |
| Special categories | None intended; Controller must not submit them |

## 3. Processor obligations

Processor will:

- process personal data only on documented instructions, including transfers,
  unless required by law (and will notify Controller unless prohibited);
- ensure personnel are bound by confidentiality;
- implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures;
- assist Controller with data-subject requests and impact assessments;
- notify Controller without undue delay after becoming aware of a personal
  data breach;
- delete or return data at the end of the agreement, subject to law.

## 4. Subprocessors

Controller authorizes the subprocessors listed in the
[privacy policy](privacy.md). Processor will give notice of new subprocessors
and impose equivalent data-protection obligations. Controller may object on
reasonable grounds.

## 5. Security measures

Encryption in transit, token authentication, least-privilege access, bounded
retention (24 hours by default for payloads), audit logging without finding
content, and documented incident response.

## 6. International transfers

Where personal data is transferred outside the Controller's jurisdiction, the
parties rely on standard contractual clauses or another lawful transfer
mechanism, as specified in an attachment.

## 7. Audit

Processor will make available information reasonably necessary to demonstrate
compliance and allow audits with reasonable notice, subject to confidentiality.

## 8. Liability

Each party's liability under this DPA is subject to the limitations in the
agreement, except where law provides otherwise.
